How to play & credits

Tap unsearched squares to find the whole paper fleet. A hit reveals a piece. Follow its neighbours to finish the boat. Finished boats reveal surrounding empty water. Two optional Pings reveal a helpful ripple. No timer or game-over penalty. Stars: finish, stay within the water budget, and use no Pings. Six / twelve stars unlock Coral / Gold seals.

Core adapted from Battleship by Harmeet Matharoo, MIT. Three.js, MIT. Original models, presentation and audio. Keyboard: Tab and Enter select tiles; Escape pauses.
